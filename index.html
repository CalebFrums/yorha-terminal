        YoRHa TACTICAL TERMINAL

        Fan-made desktop terminal in the style of NieR:Automata. Not affiliated with Square Enix. The interface is drawn in code. Characters come from the included set, or from your own folder once you choose one.

        The hub is home. Each menu entry is a place with its own sub-menu. Use HUB in the top left to come back, or leave the mouse alone and the terminal returns on its own.

        Mouse input must be allowed in Wallpaper Engine for the hub and the places to respond. Media integration feeds INTEL.
